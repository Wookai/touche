# 0003 — Kids sentences are generated once, offline

Date: 2026-10-19 · Status: accepted

## Context

Public-domain readers have dated vocabulary and attitudes and rarely fit the unlocked-key constraints (DESIGN.md, Lesson generator). An LLM can write short modern sentences inside a graded word list. CI cannot call a model: touche is public, its runners are GitHub-hosted, and Actions holds no model key.

## Decision

- Kids sentences are generated by `pipeline/` on a developer machine, through one module whose cache is keyed on (prompt, model).
- The cache, the prompts and the output are committed. Vincent reviews every sentence before it ships; the PR carries the full list.
- CI validates the committed output (schema, every word in the band's list, length cap) and never regenerates it. `touche-pipeline all` reads sentences from the cache and fails rather than call a model when an entry is missing.
- Regeneration is a deliberate, reviewed event, not a schedule.

## Consequences

Sentences change only when someone reruns the generator and reviews the result. The cache makes reruns reproducible and free.
